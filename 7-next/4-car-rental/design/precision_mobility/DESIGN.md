## Brand & Style

The platform delivers an elevated automotive rental experience centered on frictionless execution, mechanical precision, and executive confidence. The interface targets discerning travelers, business executives, and automotive enthusiasts who demand transparent pricing, premium vehicle conditions, and effortless self-service reservations.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Modern Corporate Precision** with **Atmospheric High-End Mobility**:
- Architectural structure established through clean horizontal alignments, crisp structural framing, and deliberate negative space.
- Tactile visual depth created using subtle surface luminescence, micro-gradients, and fine translucent hairline strokes.
- High-performance dashboard efficiency paired with editorial, luxury automotive catalog presentation.
- Fluid, frictionless state transitions that convey technological refinement and operational stability.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for optical clarity, trust, and distinction between operational elements and luxury accents:

- **Primary Sapphire Blue (`#1D4ED8`)**: The definitive interaction anchor. Used for primary booking triggers, selected calendar states, and dynamic status indicators. Evokes technical mastery and institutional reliability.
- **Secondary Amber Gold (`#D97706`)**: Reserved exclusively for prestige signaling—customer ratings, premier tier vehicle designations, and verification seals.
- **Tertiary Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Applied sparingly for live vehicle telemetry indicators (e.g., electric battery states, instant confirmation tags, and ambient focal glows).
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#0F172A`)**: The structural core, grounding headlines, top-level navigation shells, and high-contrast night surfaces.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas**: `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-White) provides a non-glare, pristine backdrop for imagery.
- **Elevated Canvas / Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with hairline boundaries.
- **Muted Container**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate Tone) for inactive input wells, vehicle spec chip backgrounds, and table striping.
- **Dark Inversion Elements**: `#0B1120` to `#0F172A` for dark-mode components, navigation headers, and rental agreement receipts.

## Typography

The platform utilizes a paired typographic system:

1. **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers sculpted geometric curves with humanist clarity. Tight negative letter-spacing gives headers an assertive, premium presence matching luxury automotive badging.
2. **Body & Utility Interface (Inter)**: Provides structural neutrality, high x-height, and superior small-scale legibility on data-dense specification panels.
3. **Tabular Numerics**: Set with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` for all pricing matrices, speed metrics, fuel/battery capacity levels, and countdown timestamps to eliminate visual jitter during real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows a responsive 12-column grid anchored by an 8pt architectural rhythm:

- **Desktop (≥1280px)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max content bounding container of 1440px. The standard layout pairs a persistent 280px filter sidebar with an adaptive multi-column vehicle fleet grid.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters; sidebars fold into high-performance horizontal quick-filters or drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins. Sticky booking bottom sheets ensure actionable flows remain permanently accessible.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence**: Component clusters adhere strictly to 8px base increments (8px, 16px, 24px, 40px, 64px). Form modules and vehicle metrics maintain a compact 16px gap to preserve functional data density.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established via layered tonal surfaces, micro-stroke borders, and sapphire-tinted atmospheric diffusion:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background base (`#F8FAFC`), completely flat.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `#FFFFFF` encased in a delicate 1px border stroke of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` combined with an ambient contact shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 6px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Cards)**: Scaled slightly (+0.5% scale) with elevated depth: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.05), 0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Border shifts to `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.20)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Glass Overlays & Navigation Shell)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle bottom border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modals, Date-Time Pickers & Flyout Menus)**: Deep suspended shadow: `0px 20px 35px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a Level 2 (`Rounded`) aesthetic to balance technical authority with consumer accessibility:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Spec Chips, Buttons)**: `0.5rem` (8px) radius provides defined, tailored perimeters without looking playful.
- **Containers & Vehicle Cards (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px) radius creates clean, soft-cornered viewing frames for automotive silhouettes.
- **Feature Modals & Hero Canvas Banners (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px) radius grounds primary structural zones.
- **Status Pills & Tier Badges**: Strict pill geometry (`9999px`) reserved specifically for discrete status entities (e.g., "Instant Book", "Hybrid", "Available Now").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1D4ED8` background, white label, 8px radius, height 48px (desktop) / 44px (compact), horizontal padding 24px. Hover state shifts to `#1E40AF` with a focused 3px outer ring: `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px stroke `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: No outline, text `#475569`, hover background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

### Vehicle Cards
- Dual-tier presentation: Top visual showcase featuring photography over a soft radial vignette, favorite heart toggle (floating glass circle, 36px), and automotive category pill.
- Middle tier contains vehicle name (`headline-sm`), transmission type, seating capacity, and fuel/drive-train spec badges with monochromatic icons.
- Lower pricing tier displays current daily rate in `price-display` with muted `/day` suffix, juxtaposed with a responsive primary "Rent Now" trigger.

### Vehicle Specification Chips
- Compact horizontal tags (32px height) set in `#F1F5F9` with `#475569` icons and typography (`body-sm`). Spacing between icon and value fixed at 6px. Active or highlighted vehicle perks (e.g., "Unlimited Mileage") receive a subtle cyan accent tint: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)` fill with `#0891B2` text.

### Date-Time Pickers & Search Bar Hub
- Floating dual-stage modular capsule (Pick-Up & Drop-Off) split into clean vertical cells: Location, Date, and Time.
- Active cells reveal an inline drop selector with radio check verification, calendar grids featuring selected state circular blue dots, and time dials rendered in tabular monospace formatting.

### Input Fields & Filter Controls
- Text fields utilize a 48px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, and 12px horizontal inset with floating labels or leading search/pin icons. Focus trigger shifts border to `#1D4ED8` with a 3px ambient focus halo.
- Checkboxes and Radios: Custom 20px controls with `#1D4ED8` active fill, white indicator glyph, and smooth 150ms spring transitions.

### Luxury Badging
- Ratings display amber star icons (`#D97706`) alongside numerical rating in bold tabular weight followed by review counts enclosed in muted parentheses.
