Grand Tourer Experience

The 2024 Porsche Taycan 4S represents an electrifying synthesis of legendary Zuffenhausen motorsport heritage and zero-emission luxury mobility. Engineered with permanent magnet synchronous motors on both axles and an active air suspension equipped with Porsche 4D Chassis Control, the Taycan reacts instantaneously to changing driving dynamics without sacrificing cabin composure.

Inside, the driver-centric architecture features an ergonomic sweeping 16.8-inch curved glass instrument cluster, leather-wrapped sports seats with 14-way electrical adjustment, and a dedicated 14-speaker Bose® Surround Sound system tuned specifically for the Taycan's acoustic characteristics.
